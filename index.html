03Research in focus

Devices, structures, and computational methods
MEMS / Structural optimization

Designing scanning mirrors
through computational search.

My work on MEMS scanning mirrors explores non-parametric structural design and multi-objective optimization using pixel-based genetic algorithms.

This direction connects device geometry with computational design methods. Related conference presentations cover neural-network-based linear control of PZT scanning mirrors and the design of a single-ring, dual-axis resonant piezoelectric micromirror.

Pixel-based design · Genetic algorithms · Piezoelectric MEMS

Smart structures / Nonlinear mechanics

Multiple actuators.
Coupled structural responses.

I study beam and shell structures equipped with multiple flexoelectric actuators, with a focus on modeling, optimization, actuation, and vibration control.

Published work addresses cantilever-beam optimization using Newton-Raphson iteration and shell-structure optimization using sequential quadratic programming. A current manuscript examines multi-actuator flexoelectric actuation and vibration control of parabolic shells.

Flexoelectricity · Beams and shells · Numerical optimization

Computational methods / AI-assisted design

Physics-informed tools
for engineering design.

My research interests include physics-informed neural networks, genetic algorithms, and their application to structural and actuator optimization.

I am interested in connecting mechanical models with computational search and learning methods, with MEMS and smart structures providing concrete engineering applications.

PINNs · MATLAB · Python · COMSOL Multiphysics
